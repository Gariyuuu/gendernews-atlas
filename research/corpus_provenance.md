## What a licensed corpus would add

A licensed archive of a single metropolitan daily (such as the Washington Post corpus used by the Berkeley URAP project) would remove the newspaper-composition confound, add section metadata that AmericanStories lacks, and extend coverage past 1963. The pipeline is corpus-agnostic: `src/gna/fetch.py` is the only corpus-specific module.
